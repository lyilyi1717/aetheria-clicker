How It Works: Select a seed type and plant it in any of the 16 plots. Flora grows in real time through Seed ➔ Sprout ➔ Blooming ➔ Mature, from 5 minutes (Mint Leaf) up to 2 hours (Sidr Tree). Water All adds 30s of growth (60s cooldown). Fertilize spends 1 Fresh Mint per growing plot to double that plot's next essence yield. Every harvest returns its seed and has a 25% chance to yield a mutant seed of the next tier. Garden Golems (up to 4, bought with Stone + Lemon Drops) each automate one row of 4 plots: they harvest the moment a plant matures and replant the same seed, or the row's fallback seed. Golem rows keep working offline at 50% speed for up to 12 hours.

Hasawi Lemon, Desert Truffle and Date Palm can drop Silk, Amber and Void Crystal to sell in the Bazaar.

How It Works: The Chronicle is the third prestige layer. Starting a challenge sets your current run aside; it comes back exactly as it was when the challenge ends.
