# Stronger Pick and Rafeeq Training: one button or two?

> **Evidence, not the design.** The one design file for Dig is [`docs/dig-design.md`](../dig-design.md): what was taken from this study, what is built and what the owner still decides (section 9) live there. Where this file disagrees with it, that file wins.

Study only. Nothing was changed in the game. 13 October 2026.

**Short answer.** Yes, merge them. Your feeling is right and the sim shows it: after day 1 the Rafeeqs break about 4% of the ground while you play. But the merge is not free. In a rough test the month ran about twice as fast and the Rafeeqs' sieve at the Big Rock started to pay nearly all the coins. Two things must be fixed with it.

## 1. How it works today

### Every upgrade that makes you or the Rafeeqs stronger

| Upgrade | Where | Price | What it does | Kept at a new level? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stronger Pick | Shop, coins | 10, then x1.5 each (level 10: 577, level 20: 33,000) | Your hit x1.25 | No. You start at 40% of your best level |
| Rafeeq Training | Shop, coins | 12, then x1.5 each | Each Rafeeq hit x1.25 | No. Starts at 0 |
| Hire a Rafeeq | Shop, coins | 30, 126, 529, 2,223, 9,335, 39,000, 165,000, 692,000 (8 at most) | One more Rafeeq | No. One waits for you |
| Faster Hands | Shop, coins | 250, 1,500, 9,000, 54,000, 324,000 | The beat: 100 to 160 a minute (x1.6 hits) | No |
| Second Pick | Shop, coins | 12,000, once | Twice as many hits | Yes, for ever |
| Third, fourth pick | Stars | 150, then 50,000 | x3, x4 hits | Yes |
| The Pick (Star track) | Stars | 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 23, 34 ... | Your hit x1.5 a step, plus rules (Punch-through, Heavy Hand) | Yes |
| The Camp (Star track) | Stars | the same prices | Rafeeq hit x1.5 a step, plus Rafeeqs waiting, Good Shoes, Second Kettle | Yes |
| Relics for you | Chests | | Lucky, Heavy (big hits), Boom, Echo, Long, Wide | No |
| Relics for Rafeeqs | Chests | | Karak Tea (+50% speed a level), Faz'a (they come to your block) | No |
| Wishes | Museum | | Strong Arm: your hit x2. Hot Tea: Rafeeq hit x3 | A while |

You hit about 6.7 times a second with one pick. One hit in twelve is a big hit (x5). On the Big Rock the crack counts three times. A Rafeeq hits 4 times a second when he stands still, walks between blocks, has no big hits and gets nothing from the crack. At a Big Rock every second Rafeeq sieves for coins and does not hit.

|---|---|---|---|
| Stronger Pick level | 12 | 35 | 41 |
| Rafeeq Training level | 12 | 34 | 41 |
| Rafeeqs | 3 | 8 | 8 |

The two buttons are always within one level of each other. It is one decision asked twice.

### Why the Rafeeqs feel weak while you play

On paper a Rafeeq hits almost as hard as you. In play he does not matter. Measured:

| While you play | Day 1 | After day 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Ground broken by Rafeeqs | 29% | about 4% |
| Coins earned by Rafeeqs | 23% | 3% |
| Big Rock damage by Rafeeqs | 57% | 62% |

1. **Every level starts them at zero.** You arrive with your pick at 40% of your best level. The Rafeeq arrives untrained. Example: best pick level 40. You start at level 16, a hit of 35. He starts with a hit of 1.
2. **Everything good is on your side.** Two to four picks, big hits, the crack, Punch-through, the relics. He has none of them. With the same hit, one Rafeeq is about half of one pick, less when he walks.
3. **Most ground is broken by blasts and chains**, and those count as yours.
4. **A person buys Stronger Pick first.** The sim buys the cheapest button and keeps them level. If you skip Training for ten levels, a Rafeeq hits for 11% of your hit.
5. **Their real work is time away, which you never watch.** Day 1: 71% of all coins came from time away. Days 2 to 7: 10%. After that: 3%. (The design file's 78% is from before the start kit; it is out of date.)

So "I help them more than they help me" is true everywhere except the Big Rock.

## 2. Your idea, made exact

One button: **Stronger Pick**. Rafeeq Training leaves the shop.
**A Rafeeq's hit = a share of your hit.** "Your hit" is the number in the pill: Stronger Pick levels, The Pick track, Strong Arm. Not big hits, not the crack, not the number of picks.

| Variant | The rule | Start numbers |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Flat share** | Every Rafeeq hits for the same share, always | 60% |
| **B1. The Camp sets it** | Each Camp step adds to the share | 60%, +5 points a step, 120% at step 12 |
| **B2. The crew sets it** | Each Rafeeq you hire lifts all of them | 40% with one, +5 points for each more, 75% with eight |
| **C. The Foreman's job sets it** | Each job has its own share | Beside me 60%. Follow me 100%. Go for ore 40%. Dig down 50%. Tea break: 0% while they sit, then 150% |

In all of them The Camp loses its "x1.5 a step" (section 4 says why). Price of the one button: start at 15, not 10.

## 4. Balancing

### What I tested (rough prototype, thrown away after)

Four month sims, 4 x 12 minutes player, 2 seeds. The first and last are today's game.

| | Today (before) | Merge, share 100%, price 10 | Merge, share 60% + 5 a Camp step, price 20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| First level finished | 8.2 min | 6.8 min | 10.7 min |
| 100 m Rock | day 0.8 | day 0.5 | day 0.9 |
| 150 m Rock | day 4.6 | day 3.2 | day 3.7 |
| 200 m Rock | day 13.5 | day 6.4 | day 7.8 |
| Deepest on day 30 | 250 m | 325 m | 313 m |
| Stars by day 30 | 9.9 million | 1.2 billion | 891 million |
| Hard medals won | 0 | 44 | 41 |
| Ground by Rafeeqs, all month | 4% | 14% | 17% |
| Rock by Rafeeqs, days 2 to 7 | 63% | 74% | 73% |
| Coins from time away, day 1 | 71% | 64% | 38% |
| Coins while playing that Rafeeqs earned, days 2 to 7 | 3% | 60% | 50% |
| The same, days 8 to 30 | 3% | 100% | 100% |

What this says:

- **It does what you want.** Rafeeqs go from 4% to about 15% of the ground and earn half the coins while you watch.
- **It doubles the speed of the month**, even with a lower share and a doubled price. So the cause is not the share. Price 20 only slowed the first level (10.7 minutes: too slow).
- **The coins come from the sieve.** Rafeeqs earn all the coins late in the month but break only 14 to 17% of the ground. The sieve at the Big Rock pays by how hard a Rafeeq hits, with no ceiling. A Rafeeq who arrives at a level with your strong pick prints coins there.
- **Hard medals went from 0 to 44.** My reading, not traced: hard levels were tuned around Rafeeqs who arrive weak. Now they arrive strong.

### What breaks, and the fix

| Problem | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Double scaling | If the share copies The Pick's x1.5 and The Camp still gives x1.5, Rafeeqs get both. At 6 steps each: 11 times stronger than today. At 12 each: 130 times | The Camp gives share points (B1) or rules only. Never a second multiplier |
| The sieve | Pays hit divided by ground hardness, no ceiling | A sieve hit pays at most one block of that depth |
| The start kit | The waiting Rafeeq now arrives at full strength | Good for feel. Then re-size hard levels and Gates for it |
| Time away | Follows the pick, so it grows with every press | Watch the day 1 share. If it rises, lower what a row pays while away |
| Big Rock | Rafeeq hits have no cap and no crack | 60% keeps them near 35 to 40% of the Rock. TNT they set off keeps today's cap of 12 hits |
| Second Pick, Faster Hands | They double your hits, not your hit number | Leave it so. They stay the "only me" upgrades and keep the shop a choice |
| Price | Training cost freed is worth about 1.7 pick levels | 15 to start. Not 20 |

**Tune in this order:** the sieve ceiling, then the share, then the price, then hard levels.
**Measure every time:** ground and Rock share by Rafeeqs while you play (aim: 15 to 30% ground, 35 to 50% Rock); coins from time away on day 1 and after; days to each Gate; hard medals won. The month sim does not print the first two today; they need adding.

## 5. Other things to sell, so the shop still has choices

Each one changes what you do or see. None waits on the clock; the ones you press refill by blocks you break.

| # | Name | What you do or see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pass the Pick** (top 3) | Let go of the mouse and a Rafeeq picks up your pick and keeps your beat until you come back |
| 2 | **The Whistle** (top 3) | Press it: every Rafeeq runs to your block and they all hit it together. Refills after 40 blocks |
| 3 | **Crack Spotter** (top 3) | At a Big Rock one Rafeeq points at the crack and shows where it jumps next |
| 4 | Lunch Bell | Ring it: one Rafeeq eats and rushes for his next 30 blocks |
| 5 | Coin Basket | One Rafeeq carries: ore and chests the others uncover are brought to you, nothing is left behind |
| 6 | Throwing Arm | Click a far block you can see: your pick flies to it |
| 7 | Rope Ladder | Rafeeqs may work two rows below you, so they open the ground ahead |
| 8 | Lamp Boy | One Rafeeq runs ahead with a lamp: you see two rows more of what is buried |
| 9 | Powder Boy | A Rafeeq who finds TNT carries it to where your pick points; you set it off |
| 10 | All Hands | A switch at the Big Rock: all sieve (more coins) or all hit (faster) |
| 11 | Copy Cat | When you land a big hit, the nearest Rafeeq copies it on his own block |

Why these three: each makes a Rafeeq visibly help **you**, on screen, in the minute you are playing. That is the complaint. None of them touches time away, so they are safe to add.

---

## 6. Recommendation

1. Merge the two buttons. One Stronger Pick, price from 15.
2. Start with **A: a flat 60%**. One line the player can read: "Rafeeq hit: 60% of yours".
3. Move to **B1** when The Camp is redone: the share is what The Camp sells (60% to 120%).
4. In the same change: cap the sieve, and take x1.5 off The Camp. Without both, the month doubles in speed.
5. Refund old Training levels in coins, once.
6. Add Pass the Pick, The Whistle and Crack Spotter, one at a time, each with a sim before and after.
