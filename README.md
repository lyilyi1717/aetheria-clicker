# 🌌 Aetheria: Chronicles of Eternity

**Aetheria** is a high-depth incremental / clicker game engineered to scale across **365+ days of continuous active and idle gameplay**.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start: How to Play

You can launch and play the game immediately using either of the following methods:

### Method 1: Instant Browser Launch
- Open [`index.html`](index.html) directly in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari).

### Method 2: Local HTTP Server
Run either command in your terminal:
```bash
python server.py
```
or
```bash
npm start
```
Then navigate to **`http://localhost:8101`** in your browser.

## 🎮 Non-Stop Active Activities & Game Loops

Unlike games that grind to a halt after a few minutes of clicking, **Aetheria** gives you nonstop things to do across 11 interconnected modules:

### 1. 🔮 The Primordial Monolith & Generators
- **Tactile Manual Clicking**: Click the central cosmic orb to harvest Aether with floating damage numbers and particle sparks.
- **Combo Multiplier System**: Rapid active clicking builds your combo meter up to 50x bonus yield!
- **Overcharge Frenzy Mode**: Maxing your combo triggers a 15-second screen-shaking Frenzy with auto-bursting strikes and 5x yield.
- **Golden Cosmic Anomalies**: Floating anomalies appear randomly every 60–90s; click them for massive Jackpots, instant Time Flux, or Ancient Gem veins.
- **14 Generator Tiers**: From early *Aether Tappers* to late-game *Singularity Dynamos*, *Cosmic Looms*, and *Infinity Matrices* scaling up to $10^{65}+$ Aether.
- **Milestone Upgrades**: 10, 25, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 500, 1000 ownership milestones multiply yields up to 10x!

### 2. ⚔️ The Void Tower (Active RPG Combat)
- **1000+ Procedural Floors**: Journey through 7 distinct zones (Whispering Caverns, Golem Catacombs, Sunken Arcana, Infernal Caldera, Void Citadel, Astral Nexus, and Primordial Eternity).
- **Active Click Strikes**: Don't just wait for auto-swings—click directly on monsters to strike them down as fast as your fingers can move!
- **4 Active Combat Skills**:
  - *Heavy Strike* (250% burst strike)
  - *Iron Wall* (Absorbs incoming boss attacks)
  - *Soul Siphon* (Heals 25% Max HP while dealing shadow damage)
  - *Supernova* (Devastating 500% burst devastation)
- **Loot & Equipment System**: Procedural Weapon, Armor, Amulet, and Relic drops across 5 rarity tiers (*Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Cosmic*).
- **Boss Enrage Encounters**: Floor bosses challenge you every 10 floors with a 30s enrage countdown and massive token drops!

### 3. ⛏️ Excavation & Mining Vault
- **Tactile 6x6 Digging Grid**: Chip away at rock blocks to uncover hidden veins of Gold, Ancient Fossils, and the ladder stairs down to deeper strata.
- **Strata Depths**: Dig deeper into Limestone, Granite, Obsidian, and indestructible Voidstone.
- **Gemstones**: Mine rare Rubies, Sapphires, Emeralds, Diamonds, and Void Amethysts.
- **Upgrades & Auto-Drills**: Upgrade from a Rusty Pickaxe to the Celestial Void Pick, install autonomous drills, and detonate 3x3 tiles with **Dynamite**.

### 4. 🌱 Botanical Nexus (Garden)
- **16-Plot Botanical Grid**: Plant Spore Blossoms, Mana Lilies, Solar Ferns, Frost Petals, Void Orchids, and Star Lotuses.
- **Real-Time Growth & Cross-Breeding**: Watch flora progress through Seed, Sprout, Blooming, and Mature phases.
- **Active Tending**: "Water All" for instant 25s growth surges, fertilize for double yields, and cross-breed mutants for rare hybrid seeds.
- **Harvest Essences**: Collect Spore Powder, Mana Sap, Solar Dew, Cryo Essence, and Star Nectar.

### 5. 🧪 Alchemical Crucible (Brewing & Transmutation)
- **Temporary Elixirs**: Brew potent draughts like *Titan's Draught* (+150% combat attack) and *Midas Elixir* (+300% gold).
- **Permanent Catalysts**: Synthesize permanent Hero Attack, permanent Max HP, and permanent +2% Global Production multipliers that never expire!
- **Transmutation**: Transmute raw stone into gold, and excess gold into Chrono Sand.

### 6. ✨ Arcane Grimoire (Spells)
- **6 Active Spells**:
  - *Aether Burst*: Instant 2 minutes of ambient production.
  - *Chrono Warp*: Accelerates total game speed by 5x!
  - *Midas' Blessing*: Manual clicks grant bonus gold.
  - *Celestial Alignment*: +300% Aether production for 30s.
  - *Void Cataclysm*: Deals 40% monster HP and shatters 4 mining blocks.
  - *Astral Renewal*: Instantly resets all cooldowns across all systems!

### 7. 🌌 Constellations of Eternity (Talents)
- **5 Specialization Branches**:
  - *The Way of the Clicker* (Active click yield, crit chance & crit multipliers)
  - *The Way of the Architect* (Building output, cost discounts, milestone resonance)
  - *The Way of the Warlord* (Combat power, monster gold, cosmic loot drop chances)
  - *The Way of the Artisan* (Pickaxe dig power, botanical haste, potion duration)
  - *The Way of the Chronomancer* (Max mana, offline efficiency, temporal siphon)

### 8. 📜 Adventurer's Guild Bounties (Endless Procedural Quests)
- Continuous, never-ending procedural contracts that refresh immediately upon completion.
- Fulfill diverse objectives (Clicks, Crits, Mining, Monster Kills, Boss Kills, Plants, Potions, Spells).
- Instant payouts of Gold, Chrono Sand, Guild Seals, and bonus Talent Points!

### 9. 🐪 The Grand Bazaar (Market & Caravans)
- **Simulated Commodity Stock Market**: Live fluctuating prices for Aether Ore, Mana Silk, Solar Amber, and Void Crystals.
- **Trade Trends**: Buy low during crashes and sell high during surges.
- **Merchant Caravans**: Dispatch expeditions for guaranteed 1.6x - 2.0x return on investment.

### 10. 🏛️ Dual-Layer Prestige & Metaprogression
- **Tier 1: Ascension (Cosmic Dust)**: Soft reset your earthly buildings to acquire Cosmic Dust and unlock god-tier perks in the Ascension Temple.
- **Tier 2: Multiverse Transcendence (Fracture Shards)**: Shatter reality when you achieve astronomical heights to unlock reality-altering modifiers.

### 11. ⏳ Offline Progression & The Year-Long Engine
- **Accurate Offline Simulation**: Returning after hours, days, or weeks calculates your offline production and displays a welcome breakdown modal.
- **Bankable Chrono Sand**: Absence is rewarded with bankable Chrono Sand, allowing you to trigger fast-forward time warps whenever you want active burst progression!
- **Infinite Precision BigNum Math**: Custom mantissa-exponent math supports values up to $10^{9\times 10^{15}}$, ensuring numbers never overflow to `Infinity` over years of play.
- **Save Management**: Auto-saves every 10 seconds to `localStorage`, with 1-click Export/Import Base64 strings for backups and cross-device play.
